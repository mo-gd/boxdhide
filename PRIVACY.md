# Privacy Policy

BoxdHide does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

- **Settings** are saved with `chrome.storage.sync`. Chrome may sync them across your devices through your Google account; they never reach the developer.
- **Watched status**: on Letterboxd film pages, the extension asks letterboxd.com, using your existing Letterboxd session, whether you have watched the film. The answer is only used in the page to decide what to show. It is not stored or sent anywhere else.
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or remote code.
