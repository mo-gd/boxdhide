Options

Why hide ratings?

  • No anchoring

    A high average sets expectations before the first frame. A low one makes you look for flaws.

  • No social pull

    Seeing your friends' stars nudges you toward their take before you have one.

  • No framing

    Reviews tell you what to notice. Read them after, and compare with what you saw.

What stays hidden Everything is optional and configurable

  • Ratings on by default

    The histogram and average rating.

  • Friends' ratings on by default

    Stars under your friends' avatars.

  • Review ratings on by default

    Stars next to every review.

  • Reviews on by default

    Blurred. Click one if you really want to read it.

  • Banner off by default

    The backdrop image at the top of the page.

  • Synopsis off by default

    Tagline and description.

Turn each one on or off from the toolbar popup, or pause BoxdHide entirely.

How it works

  1. Hidden from the start

    Film pages load with everything you chose already hidden. Nothing flashes on screen.

  2. Checked with Letterboxd

    BoxdHide asks Letterboxd, using your own session, whether you've watched the film.

  3. Revealed when you're ready

    Mark the film as watched and everything comes back instantly, no reload.

Privacy

BoxdHide does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

Your settings are saved with Chrome's built-in sync storage and never reach the developer. On Letterboxd film pages, the extension asks letterboxd.com, with your existing session, whether you have watched the film. The answer is only used in the page and is not stored or sent anywhere else.

No analytics, tracking, advertising or remote code.

BoxdHide is an independent project, not affiliated with Letterboxd. Released under the WTFPL.
